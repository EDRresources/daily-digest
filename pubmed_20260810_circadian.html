1. Circadian syndrome, subjective cognitive decline, and plasma biomarkers: sex-specific patterns and non-linear associations

Journal of Affective Disorders, 2026 · PMID: 42567211 · DOI: 10.1016/j.jad.2026.122346

In 2,008 cognitively normal adults from the Hubei Memory and Aging Cohort Study, circadian syndrome (a composite of metabolic, sleep, and depressive disturbance) showed a dose-response association with subjective cognitive decline, a dose-dependent association with plasma GFAP, and nonlinear associations with Aβ42/40 and NfL; effects were stronger for cognitive symptoms in men and for GFAP in women (senior author: Yan Zeng).

Rationale: largest and most direct hit of the week — a well-powered human cohort linking a circadian-relevant composite phenotype to core AD plasma biomarkers (GFAP, Aβ42/40, NfL) with a sex-stratified signal, relevant to biomarker and disparities-adjacent work even though the biomarker panel is standard and the design is cross-sectional.

Flagged, not ranked. Alzheimer's disease as a disorder of time-gated clearance: circadian regulation of glymphatic function in neurodegeneration

Experimental Neurology, 2026 · PMID: 42571787 · DOI: 10.1016/j.expneurol.2026.115959

A narrative review proposing that AD pathogenesis involves temporally misaligned glymphatic/meningeal-lymphatic clearance, driven by SCN degeneration, clock-gene disruption, and AQP4 depolarization, and arguing for circadian re-entrainment and BBB/vascular protection as therapeutic targets.
