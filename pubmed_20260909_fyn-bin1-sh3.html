1. Protein Aggregates as Drivers of Receptor Clustering and Pathological Signaling: A Framework Linking Extracellular Amyloid-β to Tau Dysregulation

ACS Chemical Neuroscience, 2026
PMID: 42690719 · DOI: 10.1021/acschemneuro.6c00331

A perspective/review proposing that amyloid-β oligomers drive clustering of a PrPc/mGluR5 receptor complex that activates Fyn, which in turn hyperphosphorylates tau; redistributed tau then recruits more Fyn postsynaptically, creating a self-sustaining tau-Fyn feedback loop that becomes independent of the original amyloid trigger. Senior author: Angelica B. W. Bold.

relevant framework, but a hypothesis-generating review rather than primary data — the proposed tau-Fyn postsynaptic feedback loop bears directly on tau pathobiology and could inform Fyn-pathway therapeutic angles, but no new experimental evidence is presented and the venue (ACS Chem Neurosci) is mid-tier; flagged for awareness rather than as a field-shifting result

2. Interpretable multimodal learning for integrating neuroimaging and genetic data in Alzheimer's disease

Frontiers in Radiology, 2026
PMID: 42707167 · DOI: 10.3389/fradi.2026.1912277

A vision-transformer/LLM model (R-GenIMA) integrating structural MRI with SNP data classified ADNI subjects across the normal-to-AD spectrum and, in its interpretability outputs, repeatedly highlighted APOE, BIN1, CLU, and RBFOX1 as stage-relevant genes with region-specific atrophy patterns. Senior author: Haoteng Tang.

low relevance — BIN1 appears only as one of four incidentally-highlighted genes in a computational imaging-genetics classifier; no new BIN1 biology, mechanism, or functional validation; included for completeness given the search hit, low priority
