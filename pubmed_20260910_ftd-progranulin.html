1. Recurrent patterns of TOP1-mediated neuronal genomic damage shared by major neurodegenerative disorders

Cell, 2026
PMID: 42385702 · DOI: 10.1016/j.cell.2026.06.013

Single-cell whole-genome sequencing of 469 neurons from C9ORF72 ALS, C9ORF72 FTD, AD, and control brains identified a shared somatic indel mutational signature (resembling ID4) linked to topoisomerase 1 (TOP1)-mediated mutagenesis, present in 76% of FTD and 61% of AD neurons but only 2% of controls. RADAR assays confirmed elevated TOP1-DNA covalent complexes, implicating TOP1-driven genome instability as a mechanism shared across TDP-43 and tau proteinopathies. Senior author: Christopher A. Walsh.

Field-shifting — top-tier journal, first cross-disease somatic mutagenesis mechanism linking FTD and AD, novel therapeutic target angle (TOP1 inhibition)

2. Fluid biomarkers in the evolving care landscape of Alzheimer's disease and related disorders

The Lancet Neurology, 2026 (Oct issue)
PMID: 42716045 · DOI: 10.1016/S1474-4422(26)00246-2

Review of blood-based biomarker progress, highlighting neurofilament light (NfL) as a clinical tool for FTD and ALS and phosphorylated tau 217 for AD diagnosis, and arguing for multimodal fluid biomarker panels to capture copathology and treatment heterogeneity. Corresponding author: Charlotte E. Teunissen.

Top-tier journal, consensus-shaping review directly relevant to biomarker strategy for FTD/AD trials

3. Tracking Socio-emotional Brain Changes in Premanifest C9orf72 Repeat Expansion Carriers: A Structural and Functional 2-Year Longitudinal MRI Study

Annals of Neurology, 2026
PMID: 42703939 · DOI: 10.1002/ana.78355

In 21 premanifest C9orf72 carriers followed over 2 years, task-based fMRI and structural MRI showed progressive thalamic atrophy and declining emotion-evoked responses in cingulate/cerebellar circuits despite stable behavioral performance, suggesting these measures as sensitive premanifest biomarkers. Senior author: Jan Van den Stock.

High relevance — premanifest biomarker data in the most common genetic FTD/ALS cause, top-tier journal

4. Progress towards a biotypic biomarker profile for amyotrophic lateral sclerosis-frontotemporal spectrum disorders

Brain, 2026
PMID: 42698276 · DOI: 10.1093/brain/awag297

Report from a 2025 international workshop proposing a "biotypic biomarker" framework for detecting ALS/FTD pathobiology (TDP-43, tau) before phenoconversion, and reviewing candidate multimodal biomarkers including cryptic-exon/cryptic-protein assays and epigenetic aging measures. Senior author: David A. Wolk.

Top-tier journal, field consensus statement on biomarker strategy for presymptomatic ALS-FTD spectrum disease

5. C9orf72-associated and sporadic FTD patient iPSC-microglia show differences in phagocytosis and gene expression

Stem Cell Reports, 2026
PMID: 42561943 · DOI: 10.1016/j.stemcr.2026.103026

iPSC-derived microglia from C9orf72-HRE and sporadic bvFTD patients both showed reduced LAMP2-A-positive vesicles versus controls, but C9-HRE microglia had distinct autophagosomal/lysosomal and phagocytic alterations and a gene-expression profile that diverged sharply from sporadic bvFTD microglia. Senior author: Annakaisa Haapasalo.

High relevance — direct overlap with Erik's iPSC-microglia model systems and TREM2/phagocytosis biology, even though mid-tier journal

6. Decoding Frontotemporal Dementia: Genes, Proteins and Prospects for Precision Medicine

Current Alzheimer Research, 2026
PMID: 42708260 · DOI: 10.2174/0115672050478591260821105132

Narrative review of FTD molecular pathology (tau, TDP-43, FUS), the MAPT/GRN/C9ORF72 genetic landscape, and emerging biomarkers (NfL, progranulin) and precision-medicine approaches including progranulin-restorative drugs and antisense oligonucleotides. Senior author: Dharmendra Kumar.

Mid-tier journal, but a useful consolidated overview of the GRN/progranulin therapeutic pipeline

7. The prevalence of frontotemporal dementia: a systematic review and meta-analysis

Neurological Sciences, 2026
PMID: 42684472 · DOI: 10.1007/s10072-026-09371-z

Meta-analysis of 21 studies (~38.7 million individuals) estimated a pooled global FTD prevalence of 30/100,000, with extreme heterogeneity across studies and a call for more population-based data from underrepresented regions. Corresponding author: Ying Huang.

Epidemiologic benchmark relevant to disparities/access-to-diagnosis work, though not mechanistic

8. A rostral prefrontal mediolateral gradient predicts creativity in frontotemporal dementia

Brain, 2026
PMID: 41614679 · DOI: 10.1093/brain/awag032

Using bvFTD as a lesion model, this study maps default-mode/executive-control network interactions within rostral prefrontal cortex and shows that the functional gradient between these networks predicts creative cognitive performance. Senior author: Emmanuelle Volle.

Top-tier journal, elegant systems-neuroscience use of bvFTD as a model, but tangential to core translational programs
