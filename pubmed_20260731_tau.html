LncRNA DLX6-AS1 Regulates the Pathological Process Related to Alzheimer's Disease via miR-204-5p

Journal: Neuromolecular Medicine, 2026
PMID: 42527713
DOI: 10.1007/s12017-026-08941-w

In serum from 133 AD patients vs. 105 controls, the long non-coding RNA DLX6-AS1 was elevated and miR-204-5p reduced, with DLX6-AS1 tracking disease severity (AUC 0.838 for AD diagnosis). In Aβ-induced neuronal injury and okadaic-acid-induced tau-hyperphosphorylation cell models, silencing DLX6-AS1 reduced tau phosphorylation, improved microtubule stability and axonal transport, and reduced neuronal apoptosis/oxidative stress via direct binding to miR-204-5p.

Rationale: modest relevance — a lncRNA/miRNA axis modulating tau phosphorylation and a candidate serum biomarker, but in cell-line models only (SH-SY5Y, Neuro-2a), no mouse or iPSC work, and a mid-tier journal. Mechanistically tangential to tau/progranulin/TREM2 biology as studied in Erik's lab; useful mainly as a biomarker-discovery data point.
