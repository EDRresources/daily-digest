1. A potent anti-Aβ-oligomer antibody (B28) reduces plaques, gliosis, and memory decline in APP knock-in mice

Cell Reports, 2026 · PMID: 42378088 · DOI: 10.1016/j.celrep.2026.117599

B28, a fully human antibody raised against aggregated synthetic Aβ and selected for neutralizing oligomer-induced tau neuritic dystrophy, markedly reduced amyloid plaques and Aβ oligomers, attenuated microgliosis/astrocytosis, preserved neurons, and lessened memory decline over four months in APP knock-in mice — outperforming lecanemab in vitro for Aβ capture affinity.

Rationale: top-tier journal (Cell Reports) plus a clean mouse-model efficacy story with a tau-relevant mechanism of action (blocking oligomer-induced tau neuritic dystrophy) — directly relevant to your translational therapeutics work even though the primary target is Aβ.

2. Meta-analysis: sTREM2 and YKL-40 track tauopathy and neurodegeneration more than amyloid

Molecular Psychiatry, 2026 · PMID: 42533036 · DOI: 10.1038/s41380-026-03787-x

Across 42 (sTREM2) and 44 (YKL-40) studies, both glial markers correlated robustly with p-tau181, total tau, and NfL (pooled r = 0.31–0.55) but only weakly with amyloid measures. Glial-tau coupling was strongest in cognitively unimpaired individuals and progressively weakened with disease progression, suggestive of glial "exhaustion."

Rationale: direct relevance to your TREM2 program — reframes sTREM2/YKL-40 as tauopathy-coupled rather than amyloid-coupled markers; top-tier journal. It's a meta-analysis rather than new primary data, but the synthesis itself is field-relevant.

3. Machine-learning classifier screens for tau PET positivity from MRI and amyloid PET

Alzheimer's & Dementia, 2026 · PMID: 42533677 · DOI: 10.1002/alz.71683

A logistic-regression model trained on ADNI (n=410) and externally validated on OASIS-3 and SCAN (combined n=297) classified Braak III/IV tau positivity from structural MRI and amyloid PET features with AUC 0.92 and 85% accuracy; MCI subjects flagged as tau-positive progressed to AD significantly faster.

Rationale: a practical, externally validated surrogate for tau PET staging — useful for trial screening and cohort enrichment; top AD-specific journal.
