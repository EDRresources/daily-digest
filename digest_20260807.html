Science / Work — Alzheimer's & FTD

Tau / Progranulin / TREM2 — New Papers (Last 24 Hours)

  • Novel large GRN deletion identified in four FTD cases missed by standard sequencing. MLPA analysis of four progranulin-related FTD patients with low plasma progranulin but no mutation on GRN sequencing uncovered a previously unreported gross gene deletion — a reminder to pursue copy-number analysis when plasma PGRN is low but sequencing is negative. PMID: 42560470.
  • Tau drives mitochondrial reverse electron transport (RET) to fuel neurodegeneration. Tau regulates RET, boosting ROS production and lowering the NAD/NADH ratio, mechanistically linking tau's physiological function to the mitochondrial dysfunction seen across tauopathies. PMID: 42561934.
  • CXCL10 marks sex-specific tau pathology progression. Spatial transcriptomics in P301S tau mice identifies CXCL10 as upregulated in regions of severe tau pathology, linking glial/T-cell neuroinflammation to female-specific disease progression in this tauopathy model. PMID: 42557563.
  • Low-cost digital single-molecule platform validated for plasma p-tau217. A DiSMS-based assay was validated across a 325-subject discovery cohort (including FTD and cognitively unimpaired controls), aiming to make large-scale plasma p-tau217 screening more accessible. PMID: 42556454.
  • Vorinostat (HDAC inhibitor) reduces tau pathology and cognitive deficits in a mouse tauopathy model. In AAV-P301L-tau mice, vorinostat improved cognition and reduced tau pathology, with effects tied to neuroinflammation and PI3K/AKT/GSK3β signaling — framed around sex differences in AD tau burden. PMID: 42556278.
  • NanoBiT screen identifies VT11 as a tau self-interaction inhibitor. A structural-complementation reporter screen of compound libraries identified the azaoxafluorene VT11 as a potent inhibitor of tau self-interaction, active in dose-response and seed-induced aggregation assays. PMID: 42552337.
  • Sex-specific resistance to amyloid-driven tau spread ("caTAUstrophe"). In 872 amyloid-positive older adults across three cohorts, sex-specific models identified distinct predictors of resistance to tau spread from medial temporal into neocortical regions, building on prior evidence of higher tau burden in women. PMID: 42557961.
  • Brain-derived p-tau217 assay tested against the continuous amyloid PET spectrum. Brain-derived p-tau217 assays were compared with conventional p-tau217 for accuracy across the full continuous range of amyloid PET burden, not just positive/negative calls. PMID: 42557955.
  • Flortaucipir tau-PET visual reads compared against CenTauR quantification. Concordance analysis between the FDA/EMA-approved visual read method for [18F]flortaucipir and the harmonized CenTauR quantitative scale for tau-PET positivity. PMID: 42562606.
