Tau / Progranulin / TREM2 — New Papers (Last 24 Hours)

According to PubMed, three new primary-research papers touching tau biology were newly indexed in the last 24 hours (no progranulin- or TREM2-specific primary papers today):

  • Parallel neuroinflammatory pathways to cerebrovascular burden and amyloid beta in Alzheimer's disease. In 126 cognitively unimpaired older adults, plasma YKL-40 tracked with white matter hyperintensity burden while plasma GFAP tracked with amyloid PET SUVR, and both independently predicted higher plasma p-tau217, which in turn predicted MTL/hippocampal atrophy and memory decline. PMID: 42569234 | DOI.
  • AI-based 3D segmentation of tangle-associated TDP-43 in neurodegeneration. Using multiplex immunostaining and AI object segmentation across AD, PART, and AD-TDP-LBD cases, mature neurofibrillary-tangle-associated TDP-43 inclusions predominated in the amygdala and CA4/dentate gyrus, revealing region-specific co-aggregation dynamics between tau and TDP-43. PMID: 42568211 | DOI.
  • Valine modulates Alzheimer's disease risk in APOE ε4 carriers. Across two cohorts, plasma valine was the only metabolite associated with reduced incident AD risk specifically in APOE ε4 carriers, correlating with greater white matter/posterior cingulate volume and lower CSF tau, with mediation partly through GFAP, NEFL, and APOE. PMID: 42567350 | DOI.
