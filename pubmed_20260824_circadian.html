1. "Like Taking Part in Star Wars": Qualitative Study Using Thematic Analysis to Explore the Acceptability and Experiences of Older Adults Participating in Remote Longitudinal Sleep and Dementia Research

Journal of Medical Internet Research, 2026 · PMID: 42611062 · DOI: 10.2196/88094

In an 8-week remote study combining actigraphy, wireless EEG, web-based cognitive tasks, and serial saliva sampling in older adults with MCI/dementia (probable Alzheimer's or Lewy body disease) versus controls, thematic analysis of interviews and questionnaires identified perceived value, trust/simplicity, and social support as key drivers of engagement with longitudinal remote sleep and circadian monitoring (senior author: Anne Roudaut).
