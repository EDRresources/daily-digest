Tau / Progranulin / TREM2 — New Papers (Last 24 Hours)

  • NAT10 inhibition corrects nuclear defects in tau-mutant human neurons and extends lifespan in a tauopathy model. In MAPT-mutant human iPSC neurons and a Drosophila tauopathy model, small-molecule inhibition of the acetyltransferase NAT10 corrected nuclear envelope deformation and nucleocytoplasmic transport defects and extended lifespan, nominating NAT10 as a therapeutic target. PMID: 42540688
  • Proteomic comparison of hippocampal neurofibrillary tangles in PART, intermediate AD and advanced AD. Laser-capture proteomics of AT8-positive tangles across 17 post-mortem brains found a conserved 63-protein tangle core plus a PART-specific "structural molecule" signature, distinct from the RNA-binding/translation signature that dominates in amyloid-positive AD cases. PMID: 42542447
  • 2-aminoethoxydiphenyl borate (2-APB) reduces tau hyperphosphorylation in a scopolamine rat model. TRPC1/calcineurin/MARK2-GSK-3β modulation with 2-APB lowered tau phospho-Ser396/Thr231, neuroinflammation and oxidative stress while improving cognition in scopolamine-treated rats. PMID: 42541674
  • AlzoSure Predict (U-p53AZ) tracks CSF tau but has only modest diagnostic accuracy. In a longitudinal CN/MCI cohort, plasma U-p53AZ correlated with CSF t-tau and p-tau181 but discriminated CN from MCI only modestly (AUC 0.62). PMID: 42540519
  • Qβ virus-like-particle vaccine targeting the tau microtubule-binding region. A bacteriophage Qβ-based conjugate vaccine against the tau MTBR elicited stronger antitau antibody titers than a KLH-based comparator (the one that reached phase 1/2), improved cognition and lowered brain tau/inflammatory cytokines in mice. PMID: 42540298
  • Plasma p-tau217 interpretation exposes limits of GPT-5 in a memory-clinic cohort. GPT-5-generated AD probability estimates diverged from Bayesian posttest probabilities using p-tau217, and physicians outperformed GPT-5 on diagnostic accuracy (81% vs. 46%); GPT-5 recommended inappropriate anti-amyloid therapy in 31% of scenarios. PMID: 42542938
  • Immunoproteasome deficiency worsens tau and amyloid pathology via a blocked microglial clearance checkpoint. In PS19 tauopathy and APP/tau knock-in mice lacking immunoproteasome subunits, microglia engulfed tau aggregates but failed to degrade them, shifting to an impaired P2ry12/Trem2 state with higher phospho-tau and amyloid burden. PMID: 42539361
  • Dual photoacoustic/NIR-II imaging probe (TREM2-ICG) tracks TREM2-positive microglia around Aβ plaques. A TREM2-antibody/ICG probe enabled multiscale, high-speed imaging of microglial chemotaxis toward Aβ oligomers in an AD mouse model. PMID: 42539584
