Science / Work — Alzheimer's & FTD

Tau / Progranulin / TREM2 — New Papers (Last 24 Hours)

  • Progranulin haploinsufficiency remodels the cerebral microvasculature and neurovascular unit (bioRxiv preprint). Chakraborty et al. show PGRN deficiency drives capillary stalling, reduced cerebral blood flow, and endothelial activation in mice, with partial conservation of these vascular signatures in human FTD-GRN patient endothelial cells — implicating microvascular dysfunction as a driver of FTD-GRN pathophysiology. PMID: 42539135
  • Scalable human neuronal models of tauopathy producing endogenous seed-competent 4R tau (Science Advances). Tsefou et al. engineered iPSC-derived neurons carrying FTD splice-site mutations that develop hyperphosphorylated, seed-competent 4R tau by day 28, creating a scalable platform for screening tau-clearance therapeutics. PMID: 42536730
  • Classification of tau status with machine learning models in amyloid-positive cohorts (Alzheimer's & Dementia; UAB ADRC co-authors). A logistic-regression model using MRI and amyloid PET features classified Braak III/IV tau positivity with AUC 0.92, and predicted tau-positive MCI patients progressed to AD significantly faster — proposed as a lower-cost surrogate for tau PET. PMID: 42533677
  • DNAJB6a deficiency induces tau pathology through IRE1α-Xbp1-induced mitochondrial dysfunction (Zoological Research). In APP/PS1 mice, loss of the chaperone DNAJB6a activated ER stress and mitochondrial dysfunction, accelerating tau hyperphosphorylation and AD-like cognitive/synaptic deficits, nominating DNAJB6a as a potential target. PMID: 42533583
  • Hippocampal neuronal progranulin mediates estrogen-deficiency-induced affective vulnerability and lysosomal-autophagic dysfunction (Molecular Psychiatry). Ovariectomized mice show reduced hippocampal PGRN and anxiety/depression-like behavior, reversible with estradiol or PGRN overexpression via restored lysosomal-autophagic flux and AMPK signaling — a mood-disorder rather than dementia readout, but relevant PGRN biology. PMID: 42533037
  • Regulation of cochlear nucleus synaptogenesis by progranulin in a stem cell-based coculture model (Stem Cells and Development). Progranulin-deficient cochlear nucleus neurons showed markedly reduced synapse formation with spiral-ganglion-like neurons in coculture, rescued dose-dependently by recombinant progranulin — an auditory-system readout of PGRN's role in synaptogenesis. PMID: 42535285
