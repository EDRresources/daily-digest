Mainstream Alzheimer's/FTD news (things patients might ask about)

  • KCL-286, a DNA-repair drug originally developed for spinal cord injury, showed in mouse studies that it repairs DNA damage, reduces inflammation, and hits multiple disease pathways rather than just amyloid or tau; it has already cleared an initial human safety trial and researchers think it could move quickly into AD testing. (ScienceDaily)
  • Data from three major Phase 3 trials are expected by year-end: AriBio's repurposed ED drug AR1001, Cognito Therapeutics' SPECTRIS brain-stimulation device, and Bristol-Myers Squibb's KarXT for AD-related agitation/psychosis.
  • Field-wide pipeline update: 158 medicines now in 192 trials, a 40% rise over the past decade, with a marked shift from amyloid-only approaches toward tau, neuroinflammation, and immune-modulating strategies.
  • FTD/GRN gene therapy: AviadoBio's ASPIRE-FTD trial of AVB-101 (gene therapy raising brain progranulin in GRN-mutation FTD) has begun dosing its fourth patient cohort, with early progranulin increases and no serious safety signals reported so far.
  • By contrast, Passage Bio closed its upliFT-D trial of progranulin gene therapy PBFT02 in May 2026 and is not enrolling further patients — a notable divergence in the GRN gene-therapy space patients may ask about.
