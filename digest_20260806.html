Science / Work — Alzheimer's & FTD

Tau / Progranulin / TREM2 — New Papers (Last 24 Hours)

  • SPP1 marks AD-specific microglia-microglia signaling. snRNAseq of postmortem AD vs. control brain identifies AD-restricted SPP1-mediated microglia-microglia crosstalk (plus GAS6-AXL signaling from inflammatory/ribosomal microglia), with RNAscope confirming SPP1+ microglia enriched near amyloid plaques. PMID: 42552722.
  • Tau PET tracks with regional cerebral hypoperfusion independent of amyloid. ADNI3 multimodal imaging (n=179) found PET tau burden negatively associated with cerebral blood flow in posterior brain regions, cross-sectionally and longitudinally, independent of amyloid-β. PMID: 42554925.
  • p75 neurotrophin receptor drives Tau-mediated neurodegeneration via RhoA/ROCK. In P301S mice, p75 mutants deficient in RhoA/ROCK signaling show reduced insoluble tau, gliosis, and neurodegeneration, and preserved spatial memory — identifying ROCK/GSK3β as downstream tau kinases modulated by p75. PMID: 42552421.
  • New iPSC-derived 3D brain tissue model recapitulates AD pathology. A reproducible human iPSC-based cortical model (neurons, astrocytes, microglia surviving 6+ months) reproduces amyloid deposition, elevated phospho-tau, and neuroinflammation; anti-Aβ immunotherapy cleared deposits and reversed glial disease signatures in the model. PMID: 42552384.
  • FibrilPaint peptides bind Tau and Huntingtin fibrils. A modular peptide platform (core unit W5P4H3R2) shows high-affinity binding across Tau and Huntingtin amyloid fibrils and can be functionalized with fluorophores or degrader-recruiting motifs. PMID: 42554508.
  • GPR146 loss boosts TREM2-dependent microglial Aβ clearance. Gpr146 knockout mice show enhanced microglial phagocytosis of Aβ42 oligomers, upregulated TREM2/GPR34/P2Y6/CR3 phagocytic receptors, and preserved BBB markers (Cldn-5, Glut-1), nominating GPR146 as a neurometabolic target. PMID: 42554945.
