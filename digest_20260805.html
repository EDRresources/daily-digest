Science / Work — Alzheimer's & FTD

Tau / Progranulin / TREM2 — New Papers (Last 24 Hours)

  • Progranulin deficiency drives maladaptive microglial states via MITF/TFE. Denali Therapeutics/UCSD team profiled myeloid cells from aged Grn-null mice and found a GPNMB+ microglial subpopulation marked by lysosomal stress and lipofuscinosis; MITF/TFE transcription factors drive this signature, and diverse lysosomal perturbations (especially deacidification) phenocopy progranulin loss, while compensatory GPNMB induction restores lysosomal acidification. PMID: 42546695.
  • Chaperone DNAJB6b suppresses tau fibril formation. Lund University group shows DNAJB6b delays tau aggregation by co-assembling with small tau aggregates and binding mature fibrils, reducing fibril growth and mass, without interacting with tau monomers (NMR-confirmed). PMID: 42547528.
  • High tau PET burden in late-onset psychosis. Japanese PET study (florzolotau) found 65% of late-onset psychosis patients were tau-positive vs. 15% of controls, with parietal tau retention independent of amyloid status and correlating with worse frontal/executive function. PMID: 42547799.
  • NanoBiT screen identifies VT11 as a tau self-interaction inhibitor. Leipzig group screened compound libraries and identified the azaoxafluorene VT11 as the most potent inhibitor of tau self-interaction in a cell-free RT-QuIC assay, without cytotoxicity or microtubule destabilization. PMID: 42552337.
  • Toll-NF-κB signaling drives tau hyperphosphorylation in Drosophila. Neuronal expression of human tau aberrantly activates Toll signaling in flies; genetic knockdown of Toll components attenuates site-specific tau hyperphosphorylation via restoration of Gsk-3β/Akt/PP2Ac signaling. PMID: 42546888.
