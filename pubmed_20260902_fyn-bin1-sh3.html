1. AGG repeat expansion and aggregation of BIN1 in multiple system atrophy

Brain, 2026
PMID: 42663379 · DOI: 10.1093/brain/awag194

Whole-genome and long-read sequencing in a familial multiple system atrophy (MSA) case identified an AGG repeat expansion in the first intron of BIN1; expansions >80 repeats were significantly enriched in pathologically confirmed MSA brains versus controls (13.4% vs 0%, p=0.003), and expansion carriers showed more BIN1-positive glial cytoplasmic inclusions, with insoluble BIN1 elevated across MSA brains regardless of repeat status. Senior author: Hideshi Kawakami.

high relevance — first causative genetic mechanism (repeat expansion + protein aggregation) for BIN1, the second-largest AD GWAS risk locus; top-tier journal (Brain); though the phenotype here is MSA rather than AD, a BIN1 aggregation mechanism is directly relevant to interpreting BIN1's role in AD-associated endocytic/synaptic dysfunction

2. Transcriptomic analysis reveals and validates lipid raft-associated biomarkers and functional networks in Alzheimer's disease

IBRO Neuroscience Reports, 2026
PMID: 42662874 · DOI: 10.1016/j.ibneur.2026.08.013

Integrative transcriptomic reanalysis of two public AD datasets (GSE5281, GSE33000) using WGCNA plus LASSO/SHAP identified 72 lipid raft-associated genes and eight cross-validated diagnostic biomarkers, including FYN, linked to reduced synaptic/oxidative-phosphorylation signatures and increased inflammatory (Th2/Treg, IL-6/IL-10) pathway activation. Senior author: Yung-Feng Lin.

modest relevance — FYN surfaces only as one of eight biomarkers in a purely computational reanalysis of existing public datasets, no new experimental validation; low-impact venue; included for completeness but not field-shifting

One record excluded: "From adolescence to Alzheimer's: a neurodevelopmental lifespan model of cognitive vulnerability" (Neurological Sciences, 2026), PMID: 42645564, DOI: 10.1007/s10072-026-09304-w. It matched the BIN1 search only because BIN1 is mentioned in passing as one of several AD genetic risk loci in a broader narrative review of adolescent neurodevelopment and later cognitive aging; it is a mid-tier-journal review, not BIN1-focused primary research or a field-defining consensus piece, and was excluded per the inclusion criteria.
