1. Microglial TDP-43 represses Tyrobp cryptic exon inclusion, disrupting TREM2-DAP12 signaling

Nature Neuroscience, 2026 · PMID: 42420559 · DOI: 10.1038/s41593-026-02348-3

In mice lacking microglial TDP-43, loss of function caused myelin engulfment defects and, mechanistically, cryptic exon inclusion in Tyrobp mRNA that truncated the DAP12 adaptor protein, thereby disrupting TREM2 signaling. Senior author: Rosa C. Paolicelli.

Rationale: top-tier journal, first mechanistic link between TDP-43 proteinopathy (the core ALS/FTD lesion) and defective TREM2-DAP12 signaling — directly bridges your FTD and TREM2 programs.

2. AD-protective PLCγ2 variant (P522R) compacts plaques and protects synapses downstream of TREM2
