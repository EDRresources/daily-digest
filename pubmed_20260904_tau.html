1. Priming of CD8+ T cells by peripheral dendritic cells exacerbates tau-mediated neurodegeneration

Nature Neuroscience, 2026 · PMID: 42693199 · DOI: 10.1038/s41593-026-02427-5

Tauopathy mice lacking cross-presenting dendritic cells are protected from neurodegeneration; peripheral dendritic-cell priming of CD8+ T cells drives brain infiltration and glial activation. Senior author: David M. Holtzman (with Jason D. Ulrich, co-corresponding).

Rationale: field-shifting and top-tier journal — establishes a peripheral adaptive-immune arm driving tau pathology, distinct from resident microglial mechanisms.

Rationale: high relevance — direct structural mechanism of tau filament pathology from a leading tauopathy lab, top-tier journal.

Rationale: field-spanning mechanism in a top-tier journal — links tau and TDP-43 pathology through a common genomic damage pathway.
