1. Progranulin deficiency drives a GPNMB+ myeloid state via MITF/TFE-dependent lysosomal stress response

Immunity, 2026 · PMID: 42546695 · DOI: 10.1016/j.immuni.2026.07.009

Multi-omic profiling of brain myeloid cells from aged Grn−/− mice identified a GPNMB-defined microglial subpopulation with lysosomal stress and lipofuscinosis, driven by MITF/TFE enhancer activation; deleting MITF/TFE reversed the progranulin-deficiency transcriptional signature, and compensatory GPNMB induction promoted lysosomal reacidification, while its loss worsened neurotoxicity. Senior author: Gilbert Di Paolo.

Rationale: top-tier journal, direct mechanistic dissection of progranulin-deficiency lysosomal biology in myeloid cells with a clear druggable node (MITF/TFE, GPNMB) — squarely in your core progranulin program.
