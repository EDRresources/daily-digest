Tau / Progranulin / TREM2 — New Papers (Last 24 Hours)

According to PubMed, three new tau-biomarker papers were newly indexed in the last 24 hours (no progranulin- or TREM2-specific primary papers today):

  • Ultrasensitive blood-based p-tau217 assay using antibody-oligonucleotide conjugates and RCA/Cas12a. A new "MPIRCA" platform converts p-tau217 immunoassay into a DNA-based rolling-circle-amplification/Cas12a readout, achieving a 1 fg/mL limit of detection — a substantial sensitivity gain for blood-based AD diagnostics. PMID: 42567574 | DOI.
  • Plasma p-tau217/Aβ42 ratio interacts with white matter hyperintensities to predict cognitive decline in nondemented adults. In 463 ADNI participants, p-tau217/Aβ42 was the only plasma biomarker that consistently interacted with WMH burden across cognitive domains and progression risk, with GFAP partially mediating the effect — strongest in cognitively normal men. PMID: 42568064 | DOI.
  • Genetic architecture of plasma p-tau181 and p-tau217 in twins. In 696 twins (ages 65-85), heritability ranged from 0.39 (p-tau181) to 0.66 (GFAP), with p-tau181/p-tau217 showing the strongest genetic and environmental correlation (though not unity) — suggesting partly distinct genetic drivers for the two tau species. PMID: 42566983 | DOI.
